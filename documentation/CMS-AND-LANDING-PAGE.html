1. Opening the CMS

  1. Open https://srrcareers.in/admin.
  2. Sign in with your admin account.
  3. Use the left sidebar:
Sidebar group What you edit
Global Header, footer, contact, social — shared on every page
Website Pages Landing page and other page layouts
Collections Images, course pages, workshop pages, blog posts

2. Landing page (Home)

The homepage at https://srrcareers.in is edited under Globals → Home Page. Tabs follow the page from top to bottom.

Tab Section on srrcareers.in
1 · Hero Top banner: label, headline, two buttons, large photo
2 · Problem “Does this relate…” heading and six pain-point boxes
3 · Mission “Our Mission” heading and stats / alumni cards
4 · Instructor Instructor photo, bio, and feature bullets
5 · Curriculum Module list and syllabus button
6 · Testimonials Student quotes
7 · Book a demo Bottom blue “book a demo” band

Hero tips

  • Main headline and Highlighted headline are the two title lines.
  • Primary (gold) and Secondary (outline) buttons each need text + a link (e.g. /courses or #book-demo).
  • Hero photo — choose from Media, or leave empty for the default.
  • Hero photo description — short alt text for accessibility.

After editing: Click Save (top right), then refresh srrcareers.in to confirm the change.

3. Images

  1. Go to Collections → Media.
  2. Upload a file and fill in Image description.
  3. Return to the page global and select that file in the photo field (Hero photo, Instructor photo, etc.).

5. Google Sheets (leads & enrollments)

Website forms write into one Google Spreadsheet. Use this sheet to review demo leads, newsletter sign-ups, paid enrollments, internship applications, and Join Our Team requests.

Tabs inside the sheet

Tab name What lands there

Tip: Open the matching tab after testing a form on the site. New rows appear at the bottom. Enrollment status colours help spot Success / Pending / Failed quickly.
